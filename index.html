How to present

  • Each day is a webpage. Open it, hit F (or F11) for 1920×1080.
  • Space / Right / Enter next. Left / Backspace back. Home / End.
  • S or P opens presenter notes (current, next, elapsed). Keep that off-camera.
  • Click advances; right-click goes back. Esc leaves fullscreen.
  • Keycaps are keys. Talk. The slides stay sparse on purpose.
