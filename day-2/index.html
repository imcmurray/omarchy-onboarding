Day 2 · Fifteen minutes

Navigation.

Everything happens via the keyboard. Everything.

The default

Dwindle: every window stays visible.

Open more, they shrink. Nothing hides under anything. That's the whole idea.

Split direction

Stack, or sit side by side.

Toggle splitSuper+J
Swap windowsSuper+Shift+Arrow

Focus

Look with the arrows.

FocusSuper+Arrow

Focus moves, and the cursor jumps to the center of the new window.

Workspaces

Spaces you'll actually use.

JumpSuper+1 2 3 4
Take a windowSuper+Shift+1 … 4

No animation delay. Instant. Super+Shift+Alt+number sends it without following.

Scratchpad

A Quake console for any window.

ToggleSuper+S or Super+`
Send thereSuper+Alt+S or Super+Shift+`

An overlay on whatever workspace you're on. Perfect for an agent terminal.

Groups

Tabs, without the browser.

GroupSuper+G
Inside the groupSuper+Ctrl+←/→

New windows join the group while it's active. Super+G again tears it down. Super+Alt+G plucks one out.

Pop

Pin a window. Take it with you.

Sticky floatSuper+O

Video, a call, a reference pane — it follows you across workspaces.

Tile, float, fill

Tile / floatSuper+T
FullscreenSuper+F
Full widthSuper+Alt+F

Super+Ctrl+F is fullscreen inside a window — the YouTube key.

The other layout

Dwindle, or a sideways strip.

Toggle layoutSuper+L

Scrolling lines windows up beyond the edge. Per workspace, and it remembers. Default lives in looknfeel.lua.
