Day 1 · Intro

This is an adventure, not a BIOS screen.

You're here because something about a beautiful, keyboard-first Linux looked like a dare.

What it is

Omakase Linux.

  • Arch + Hyprland + Quickshell
  • By DHH — chef's choice, not a buffet
  • Neovim, Chromium, Obsidian, LibreOffice
  • Kdenlive, OBS — even a Winamp-style player

Mindset

Not Mac. Not Windows. On purpose.

It isn't trying to be familiar. It's trying to be beautiful and better. Embrace the Linux-ness.

Why it looks like this

A beautiful system is a motivating system.

Productivity has always been downstream from motivation.

The one key

Super is the center of everything.

Super is the Windows key. On a Mac keyboard, it's where Cmd always was.

The menu

Your new Spotlight.

Omarchy menuSuper+Space
Apps onlySuper+Alt+Space

Start typing. Apps, settings, install, capture — just about everything.

The cheat sheet

The only hotkey you have to memorize.

All bindingsSuper+K

Blank on a key? Ask the machine. Tmux: Super+Alt+K. Herdr: Super+Ctrl+K.

Tiling, first glimpse

Open two things. Watch them share.

TerminalSuper+Return
BrowserSuper+Shift+Return

Windows place themselves. You don't fish one out from under another.

Leaving a window

Close means quit.

Close windowSuper+W or Super+Q

No Mac limbo. Super+Q is there for the finger memory you arrived with.

If you're still installing

ISO. Then three gotchas.

  • Secure Boot / TPM off in BIOS
  • Wired or 2.4 GHz keyboard for disk encryption
  • Bluetooth won't type the LUKS password at boot

Stuck

Discord. #omarchy-help.

Someone is usually willing. That's the official backstop, not a forum archaeologist.

Recap

Four keys. That's Day 1.

BrowserSuper+Shift+Return

Homework

Tonight, muscle memory.

  1. Open the menu. Type something. Leave.
  2. Terminal, then browser. Watch them tile.
  3. Hit Super+K. Scroll. Don't memorize.
