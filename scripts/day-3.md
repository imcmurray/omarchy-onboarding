## 4. Clipboard

*[stage] Demo in the terminal if you can. Super+C/X/V everywhere. Super+X cut except terminal. Super+Ctrl+V history.*

### You say

Linux usually wants Control plus Shift plus C in the shell, and Control plus C everywhere else, and Control plus C in the shell is interrupt, and that folklore has stolen more copied commands than any adversary. Omarchy gives you Super plus C, Super plus X, Super plus V everywhere. One family. The Super key you have been living on.

Super plus C copies. Super plus V pastes. Super plus X is cut — except in the terminal, because a terminal has nothing to cut, it has a stream. Copy still works there. Super plus C in foot, paste Super plus V in the browser. Believe it. That is homework later, and it is the conversion moment.

Super plus Control plus V is history. Text and images. That is Windows plus V if you came from Windows, and it is the thing Mac people keep installing a utility to get. Select a clip, Return, then Super plus V. You will use this more than you think, especially after we take a screenshot.

Default terminal is foot. The binding follows whatever terminal you install. The Super plus C rule does not change.

## 5. Capture
**Clock:** 4:51–6:14 · **194 words · 83s**

*[stage] Print Screen freeze+region; PNG disk+clipboard; click notification to annotate; Alt+Print Screen records; Super+Ctrl+C if no Print Screen. OCR/dictation: one line, later.*

### You say

Print Screen freezes the screen. The whole thing pauses, like the machine took a breath. Drag a region, or click a window. You get a PNG on disk and on the clipboard. Super plus V pastes it. That is not a maybe. That is the design.

Click the notification if you want to annotate. Circle the bug. Arrow the button. Send it to the person who caused it.

Alt plus Print Screen records. It asks which audio — none, desktop, plus mic, the grown-up choices — then the same picker as the screenshot. Region or window. Hit Alt plus Print Screen again to stop. Thirty seconds is plenty for homework. You are not making a documentary.

No Print Screen key? Some laptops hide it under Fn, some keyboards pretend they are too pretty for it. Super plus Control plus C is the capture menu. Same camera, summoned with Super.

If you need them later: OCR is Super plus Control plus Print Screen, dictation is Super plus Control plus X. I am not teaching those tonight. They exist. Super plus K will still be there when a screenshot of text starts feeling like a waste.
